Quantum DCMS

A sports-rights contract management dashboard — built so account teams can answer "who owns this deal and what's it worth" without a data analyst in the loop.

The Challenge

Deals, rights, and sponsors, three different ways

Sports-rights contract data has to be sliced by geography, sport, deal type, and counterparty — often for different audiences asking different questions in the same meeting. Client account teams needed to answer those questions directly, not route every query through a data or reporting specialist.

The Decision

Make relationships the primary filter, not an afterthought

I structured the dashboard around three relationship types — rights holders, sponsors, and broadcasters — as first-class filters alongside a query builder, rather than burying them as report parameters. That makes "show me every deal with this broadcaster across territories" a two-click question instead of a spreadsheet request.

What I Owned
  • Information architecture — defined how organisations, deals, and reports relate to each other in the data model that drives the dashboard
  • Prototype build — coded the organisation dashboard, query builder, and reporting views as a clickable prototype
  • Stakeholder framing — designed the dashboard to serve account teams directly, rather than as a report-request queue for a central data team
