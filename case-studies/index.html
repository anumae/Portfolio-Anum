Case File — 02 · CBUAE-Regulated Digital Banking

YAP Digital Banking

Role: Senior PM / Delivery Lead — owned the central admin onboarding portal, compliance-critical data routing, and UAT resolution

Problem

A branchless, CBUAE-regulated digital bank needed to onboard customers at scale using OCR-extracted identity documents, while guaranteeing data privacy, secure third-party API integration, and system stability under high concurrent load — a first-attempt, regulator-facing go-live with zero margin for compliance error.

Approach

  • Owned the central admin portal managing customer onboarding status and sensitive identity data
  • Enforced OCR data validation to prevent information leakage during transaction processing
  • Implemented API retry logic to prevent silent failures in third-party routing
  • Built load balancing into the architecture to hold stability under high onboarding volume
  • Drove UAT resolution on Emirates ID extraction accuracy and correct bank-routing logic ahead of go-live
  • Supported a no-fee, flexible-FX onboarding offer to drive early adoption

Outcome

1,000 customers onboarded at launch First-attempt, CBUAE-regulated go-live Onboarding time: 4–6 days → 3 days Physical card delivery: ~1 week → next-day virtual issuance

Case File — 05 · Retail & Hospitality Inventory SaaS

StokkedPro

Role: Product Owner / Project Management & Analysis — owned solution architecture and OCR-based invoice automation

Problem

US retail and hospitality brands needed a multi-brand, multi-branch inventory and stock management system — with invoice processing still handled entirely by hand across brands.

Approach

  • Built a multi-tenant structure: each brand supports multiple branches, each managing its own stock, inventory, orders, and dispatch
  • Delivered OCR-based invoice extraction with brand-specific dynamic field mapping
  • Designed confidence-score routing: extractions at or above 80% confidence sync automatically to third-party systems; lower-confidence extractions route to human moderation
  • Delivered a modern, user-friendly UX to support fast client onboarding and adoption

Outcome

Pilot client onboarded for POC launch Scaled to 3 major clients 80%+ of invoices auto-processed, no manual touch [X] minutes saved per invoice

Case File — 06 · Governance Function, Built From Zero

Building the PMO at Mayfair

Role: Founder of the function — 13 direct reports, informal oversight of 40+, across 5 projects and products
