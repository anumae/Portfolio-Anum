YAP Digital Banking

Digitify Ltd. — governing a digital bank's first production go-live inside a CBUAE-regulated environment, across a 50–60 person cross-functional team.

This was a production release inside a regulated banking environment — there's no public interactive prototype for this one, unlike the AI/SaaS projects above.

The Challenge

Four workstreams, one regulator, one go-live date

IBAN provisioning, payments, customer onboarding, and compliance were being built by separate teams on separate timelines, but they all had to land together for a single production go-live — inside a Central Bank of the UAE regulated environment, where a rollback isn't a quiet redeploy, it's a regulatory event.

The Decision

RAID logs as the sign-off authority, not a status report

With four workstreams each capable of independently declaring themselves "ready," I treated the RAID log as the single source of truth for go/no-go — sign-off required cross-workstream evidence that dependencies were actually closed, not just each team's individual confidence. In a regulated environment, that distinction is the difference between a go-live and an incident report.

What I Owned
  • Requirements workshops — ran these directly across IBAN, payments, onboarding, and compliance workstreams
  • RAID log and stakeholder reporting — maintained as the cross-workstream readiness record throughout SIT/UAT and release
  • Team coordination — coordinated a 50–60 person cross-functional team through SIT/UAT and into production release
  • Commercial support — supported the YAP Pakistan commercial case alongside the core delivery work
