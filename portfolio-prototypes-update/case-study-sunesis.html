Sunesis

An AI-enabled, multi-tenant LMS platform — taken from a client's raw MVP scope and AI R&D workbooks to an approved roadmap, a set of shipped modules, and a fully coded prototype used in client review sessions.

The Challenge

An AI wishlist, not yet a roadmap

The client came in with an Excel MVP scope and a separate AI R&D workbook — a long list of things the platform could theoretically do (assessor dashboards, at-risk learner flagging, AI feedback, onboarding chatbots, and more) but no sequencing, no PRD, and no clear line between "MVP" and "later." Fourteen AI features were in scope on paper. None were prioritized.

The Decision

Scope the 4, roadmap the rest

Rather than negotiate the wishlist down feature-by-feature, I split it structurally: 4 AI features became the MVP (learner onboarding chatbot, AI feedback engine, at-risk flagging, personalized learning path generator), and the remaining 10 were sequenced into a phased roadmap presented to the board. This protected the go-live date from scope creep while giving the client a credible answer for "what happens to everything else" — instead of either cutting features silently or trying to build all 14 at once.

What I Owned
  • Requirements translation — converted the client's Excel MVP scope and AI R&D workbook into 1 master product plan and 7 part-level PRDs, in a format engineering could actually build from
  • Board-level buy-in — built the report and presentation deck that got the phased AI roadmap approved, covering the 4 MVP features and the 10-feature backlog
  • Prototype build — coded the interactive prototype myself (single-file app, collapsible sidebar navigation, brand-consistent design system) so the client could click through the assessor dashboard, training-record views, and platform-wide metrics before a single line of production engineering started
  • Client review facilitation — ran the prototype through client approval sessions, incorporating feedback ahead of the engineering handoff
Outcome
