HRMS / HR-Tech · UAE HRIS Modernization

Beneple (BSTG)

A modernized HRIS covering the employee lifecycle — onboarding, salary, recruitment, and training — sequenced around adoption before analytics.

The Challenge

Four modules competing for the first release

Onboarding, recruitment, leave management, and insurance administration were all in scope for modernization, each with its own stakeholder group (HR ops, line managers, employees themselves) and its own definition of "done." Building all four with equal depth in one release risked shipping four half-finished modules instead of one that actually worked end-to-end.

The Decision

Adoption first, reporting depth second

I sequenced employee-facing self-service workflows — leave requests, recruitment status, insurance enrollment — ahead of deeper HR analytics and reporting. The reasoning: a modernized HRIS only pays off once employees actually use it day-to-day; reporting depth is only valuable once there's real usage data to report on.

What I Owned
  • Module sequencing — prioritized leave request, recruitment, and insurance self-service flows for the initial release
  • Prototype build — coded the employee dashboard covering headcount, retention, pending requests, and the interactive leave/recruitment/insurance demo flows
  • Lifecycle mapping — structured the platform around the full employee lifecycle rather than a single HR function in isolation
Outcome
3
Self-service workflows shipped first: leave, recruitment, insurance
1
Unified employee lifecycle system, replacing siloed processes
